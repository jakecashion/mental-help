# Locate and Prepare ship before Orient

Orient is the first phase in the user funnel but the last to ship. The decision tree powering Orient cannot go in front of real users without clinical validation from an advisor — that dependency is unresolved at the start of build. Locate and Prepare have no clinical dependency and deliver real value independently: a caregiver who already knows what care level they need can go straight to Locate. Shipping in reverse funnel order (Prepare → Locate → Orient) ensures visible progress and a usable product while the clinical advisor engagement is pursued.
